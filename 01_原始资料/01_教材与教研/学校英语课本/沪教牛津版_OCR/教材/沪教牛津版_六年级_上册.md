Moving

Every day, people move to new places. Why do people move? Let's look at three important reasons.

**Jobs**

Cities often have more businesses, so there are more jobs for people. In 2001, around 38 per cent of people in China lived in cities. In 2022, that number was around 65 per cent.

> Last year, my parents found new jobs in Chengdu, so we moved here. I'm in a new school, and I like my new friends.

### Explore

**Forces and movement**

We see things moving every day. We see people walking, birds flying and leaves falling. What makes things move?

**Forces**

A force can make something move. A push is a force. When we push, it moves people or things away from us. A pull is a force too. When we pull, it moves people or things towards us.

**Movement**

A small force causes a small movement and a big force causes a big movement. When we use a small force to throw a ball, it does not go far. When we use a big force, it goes a long way. Also, it is easier to move a light thing than a heavy thing.

**Speed**

Speed is how fast things move. Some things, like trains, move fast, and some things, like tortoises, move slowly. A car is faster than a horse, a high-speed train is faster than a car, and a plane is the fastest of all.

A force can change the speed of things. When you push a door with a small force, it opens slowly. When you push it with a bigger force, it opens more quickly.

> Next time you see something moving, ask yourself, "What force is moving it, a push or a pull? Is the force small or big?"

### Extend

**From town to megacity**

The year is 1979. A small town sits at the mouth of the Pearl River. Nothing much happens there. The buildings are small. Only about 310,000 people live there. It is Shenzhen.

It is 1980. Many new businesses are opening in Shenzhen. Thousands of young people come here. They begin to build tall buildings.

It is now 1990. About 1.68 million people live in Shenzhen and more workers are coming. They work hard in new factories. They have a better life in Shenzhen.

Ten years later, the population jumps to about 7 million!

Shenzhen keeps growing. Today, it is full of modern buildings and green spaces. It has more than 1,000 parks! About 12 million people live in Shenzhen. They make this city a great place.
