## Welcome 与 Unit 1 Me!（我自己）· 活动练习

### 第 3 页篇首（Welcome back to Pippa and Pop）
- **Hello again!** Circle. Look. Find.

### 第 4 页（Welcome）
- **Look. Match. Say.**（欢迎回到 Pippa and Pop）

### Unit 1 Me!（Unit 1 起始于本书第 5 页）
- **Listen again. Look. Stick. Point.**（Unit topic introduction: Song practice，书内第 6 页标号）

### 词汇与语言练习
- **Look. Match. Say. Count.**，活动含 Ask a friend: `What's your name? How old are you?`（附「At home」栏：请与朋友互相问答）
- **Circle. Say. Look. Find.**
  - `What's your/her/his name? I'm/She's/He's (Pippa/Dan/Kim/Tinks/Pop). How old are you/is she/is he? I'm/He's/She's (six/five/eight/three).`

### Sounds and letters（字母音复习）
- **Circle. Say. Listen again. Look.**
- 复习字母音：b, m, t, g, p, d, k, n, s, h（字母迷宫/方阵练习）

### Literacy practice
- **Circle. Look. Point.**（Jane's name）

### Values（价值观）
- 主题：**Be yourself**（做自己）
- **Look. Find. Draw.**
- 活动栏目（At home）：`What do you like about yourself?`

### 情绪词汇练习
- **Say. Look. Find. Circle.**
- 词汇：excited, sleepy, angry, surprised, scared, bored

### 语言练习
- **Look. Find. Say. Match.**
- `She's/He's/I'm (sleepy/surprised/scared/bored/excited/angry). She isn't/He isn't/I'm not (bored).`

### Numbers（数字）
- **Look. Count. Say. Draw.**
- 复习数字：1–20（数点阵图）

### Music（音乐）
- **Listen again. Draw.**
- 活动栏目（At home）：`Listen to some music together. Say how it makes you feel.`（Emotions from music）

### Review（复习）
- **Look. Say. Draw.**
- `I'm/She's/He's (surprised). I'm not/He isn't/She isn't (bored).`

### Unit 1 收尾 Review
- **Point. Say. Draw.**

## Unit 2 My day（我的一天）· 活动练习

### 主题导入

### 词汇与语言练习
- **Look. Say. Match.**
- **Say. Look. Find. Circle.**
- `I (wash my face/brush my teeth/wake up/get dressed/brush my hair/have breakfast) (in the morning/every day).`

### Sounds and letters（字母音）

- 字母音 /ʃ/（sh）

### Literacy practice

### Values（价值观）
- 主题：**Look after yourself**（自我照料）
- **Look. Trace.**
- 活动栏目（At home）：`How do you look after yourself?`

### 晚间活动词汇练习
- **Say. Look. Find. Circle.**
- 词汇：play with friends, have a snack, have a bath, listen to a story, go to bed, have dinner

### 语言练习
- **Say. Look. Find. Circle.**

### Numbers（数字 · 加法）
- **Say. Look. Count. Match.**
- 主题：Adding up by counting（含补全加法算式）
- 活动栏目（At home）：`Make a new adding question.`

### Social studies（社会研究）
- **Look. Circle. Say.**
- 主题：**Times of day**（一天中的时段）

### Review（复习）
- **Look. Say. Draw.**

### Unit 2 收尾 Review
- **Point. Say. Draw.**
