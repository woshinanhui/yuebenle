## 正文概览（Map of the Book）

练习册前几页为「课时地图（Map of the Book）」，各单元能力目标与学生用书一致，列出（供参考）：
- **Welcome / Hello**：Unit 1 前为 Welcome（原书第 4–5 页）。
- **Unit 1 My Friends**：词汇 book, crayon, pencil；Pippa, Pop, Dan, Kim；语言 Hello. I'm (Pippa). / What's this? It's a (pencil).；区分音；文学 Duck's friend；数字 1、2；社会 Sharing；价值 Be friendly。
- **Unit 2 My Family**：词汇 brother, sister, daddy, mummy；boy, girl, man, woman；语言 She's the (mummy). He's my (brother). He's/She's a (boy).；文学 The big carrot；数字 3、4；科学 How food grows；价值 Help your family。
- **Unit 3 My Toys**：词汇 ball, doll, teddy, train；blue, brown, red, yellow；语言 It's a (ball). It's (red).；区分字母与实物；文学 Big teddy, small teddy；数学 Big and small；价值 Celebrate differences。
- **Unit 4 My Body**：词汇 ears, eyes, mouth, nose；arms, feet, hands, legs；语言 Touch your (nose). Colour the (arms).；文学 Bunny's family；社会 Feelings；价值 Be kind。
- **Unit 5 Food**：词汇 apples, bananas, biscuits, sandwiches；juice, milk, water；语言 I like (apples). I don't like (juice).；字母音 a；文学 Picky Peter；数字 5、6；科学 Identifying fruit；价值 Say thank you。
- **Unit 6 Animals**：词汇 cat, dog, fish, rabbit；chair, table，on, under；语言 Where's the (cat)? Here it is. It's (under) the (chair).；字母音 e；文学 Emma's new cat；数字 7、8；科学 What animals need；价值 Be kind to animals。
- **Unit 7 Clothes**：词汇 hat, jacket, shoes, socks；dress, skirt, trousers, T-shirt；语言 (Put on/Take off) your (hat). I've got (a dress).；字母音 i；文学 Tommy's T-shirt；形状 circle, square, triangle；美术 Rough and smooth；价值 Be considerate。
- **Unit 8 Transport**：词汇 bike, bus, car, van；drive a car, jump, ride a bike, run；语言 I can see a (car). I can (ride a bike).；字母音 o；文学 The hare and the tortoise；数字 9、10；科学 Fast and slow；价值 Be careful。
- **Unit 9 The Park**：词汇 bird, flower, frog, tree；green, pink；butterflies, caterpillars, ladybirds, worms；语言 A (green) (tree). What are these? They're (butterflies).；字母音 u；文学 Penny in the park；数字 One more；数学 Symmetry；价值 Look after nature。

## Unit 1 · My Friends

### 单元引入（歌曲练习）

- Say. Look. Trace. Colour.
- **词汇**：book, crayon, pencil。

### 语言练习 · 文具
- Say. Look. Find. Colour.

### 数字 Numbers: 1, 2
