## 2 Brush Your Hair, Leo!

### 1 (第 8 页)
This is Leo. He's five. Leo is a very good boy.
He's got a mummy, a daddy and a baby sister. But ... look at his hair!
Brush your hair, Leo!

### 2 (第 8 页)
"I wake up in the morning. I wash my face and I get dressed. I tidy my bedroom.
But Leo—brush your hair!"

### 3 (第 9 页)
Mummy: "Leo, brush your hair! It's time for school."
Leo: "I have breakfast. I brush my teeth. I put on my jacket."

### 4 (第 10 页)
Mummy: "Leo, brush your hair!"
Leo: "Bye, Mummy!"
Leo goes to school.

### 5 (第 11 页)
Leo is in the garden. He's playing with his boats.
But where's the green boat?
Mummy: "Leo, your hair!"

### 6 (第 11 页)
Leo can feel something in his hair.
Leo: "What is it?"

### 7 (第 12 页)
It's a green boat!
Leo: "I don't like boats in my hair."
Mummy: "Well, Leo, brush your hair!"

### 8 (第 12 页)
"I'm a good boy. Every day I wash my face. I brush my teeth.
And ... I brush my hair!"

## 3 Goldilocks and the Three Bears

### 1 (第 13 页)
Mummy Bear, Daddy Bear and Baby Bear live in a house in the forest.
Mummy Bear cooks porridge for breakfast.
Mummy Bear: "Oh! The porridge is very hot. Let's go for a walk."

### 2 (第 13 页)
Goldilocks sees the bears' house. She looks and she listens. It's quiet. She goes in the house.
Oh, Goldilocks! It isn't your house!

### 3 (第 14 页)
Goldilocks sees the porridge. It smells good.
"Mmmm..."
Goldilocks tastes Daddy Bear's porridge.
"It's very hot." → 图说: 哎呀，太热了![?]

### 4 (第 14 页)
Goldilocks tastes Mummy Bear's porridge.
"Ew" — It's very cold.
Goldilocks tastes Baby Bear's porridge. It tastes good.
"Mmmm ... yummy!
Oh, Goldilocks! It isn't your porridge!"

### 5 (第 15 页)
Oh, Goldilocks! It isn't your chair!
Goldilocks sits on Baby Bear's chair. Uh-oh!
She sits on Mummy Bear's chair. Hmph. "It feels very hard."
Goldilocks sits on Daddy Bear's chair. Hmmm. "It feels very soft."

### 6 (第 16 页)
Goldilocks tries Daddy Bear's bed.
Oh, Goldilocks! It isn't your bed!
She tries Mummy Bear's bed.
Goldilocks tries Baby Bear's bed.
Goldilocks is sleepy. Ahhh...

### 7 (第 17 页)
Uh-oh. The bears are home.
Baby Bear: "Where's my porridge? Look at my chair!"
Baby Bear: "Who's in my bed?"

### 8 (第 17 页)
Goldilocks wakes up. She hears the bears!
Goldilocks: "Oh! I'm very sorry. I can make the beds. I can wash up!"
Three bears: "OK, thank you, Goldilocks."
Mummy Bear: "Let's have breakfast."
Daddy Bear: "I can make some more porridge."

## 4 A Sport for Grace

### 1 (第 18 页)
Grace is bored. Her brother isn't at home. He's playing basketball.
Her sister isn't at home. She's playing hockey.
Her daddy isn't at home. He's playing tennis.
Grace: "Hmph. I'm bored."

### 2 (第 18 页)
Grace: "Mummy, I like sports. Can I play a sport?"
Mummy: "Hmmm ... OK. Let's go to the park."
Grace: "Yes! Thank you, Mummy!"

### 3 (第 19 页)
Grace is playing basketball with her brother.
The ball is big. The children are tall. Grace is short.
Grace: "I don't like basketball."
Grace is sad.

### 4 (第 20 页)
The girls are fast. Grace is slow. Grace is sad.
Grace: "I don't like hockey."

### 5 (第 21 页)
Grace is playing tennis with her daddy.
The racket is big. Grace is small. Grace is sad.
Grace: "I don't like tennis."

### 6 (第 21 页)
Grace: "I'm sad. I can't play basketball. I can't play hockey. And I can't play tennis!"
Daddy: "Grace, I've got an idea! Let's go!"

### 7 (第 22 页)
Grace is in the sports centre.
There are small children. They're playing football. They're happy.
Grace: "Oh! Can I play?"

### 8 (第 22 页)
Grace is playing football.
Grace isn't sad. Grace is happy.
Mummy and Daddy: "Go, Grace!"
Grace: "I love football!"

## 5 Jack Loves Reading

### 1 (第 23 页)
Jack likes reading books.
He reads books in the morning. He reads books at night. Jack loves reading books.

### 2 (第 23 页)
Jack is reading a book now. It's a story about a superhero. His name's Power Hero.
Jack likes superheroes. Superheroes help their family and friends.

### 3 (第 24 页)
Look! It's Cookie Blob! Cookie Blob is very bad. He loves eating cookies. He's eating the children's cookies!
"Jack! Jack, come and help, please!"
Oh! That's Jack's mummy.

### 4 (第 24 页)
The children are sad. The teacher is angry.
Look! It's Power Hero! Power Hero can help.
"Jack! Jack, please set the table!"

### 5 (第 25 页)
Power Hero has got a big cookie.
Power Hero: "Look, I've got a big cookie!"
Cookie Blob: "Cookie, cookie, cookie!"
Jack's Mummy: "Jack! Come and help, please, Jack!"[?]
Look! There's a balloon with lots of cookies!

### 6 (第 26 页)
The children are happy. The teacher is happy.
Cookie Blob is flying away in the balloon.
Cookie Blob: "Cookie! Cookie! Cookie!"
Power Hero: "Goodbye, Cookie Blob!"
And Jack is happy. He loves his book. But Jack's mummy isn't happy.
Jack's Mummy: "JACK!!!!"

### 7 (第 27 页)
Jack loves reading. Jack loves superheroes. Superheroes help. But...
Jack's Mummy: "JACK!"
Jack isn't helping his mummy.
Jack's Mummy: "Jack!"

### 8 (第 27 页)
Please help. Please set the table.
Jack: "Sorry, Mummy. Superheroes help. I can help, too."
Jack's Mummy: "Good boy, Jack. Let's go."

## 6 Share, Ricky Raccoon!

### 1 (第 28 页)
Ricky Raccoon loves sweets. Look - he's got lots of sweets. Ricky is eating the sweets.
But the little animals like sweets, too. The animals are sad.
Share, Ricky Raccoon! Share!

### 2 (第 28 页)
The animals are having a picnic. They're playing games. But not Ricky.
Ricky is eating the ice cream.
Oh no, Ricky! Share, Ricky Raccoon! Share!

### 3 (第 29 页)
Oh dear, Ricky!
Animals: "Ricky Raccoon! Where's our ice cream?"
The little animals are sad.

### 4 (第 30 页)
Oh no, Ricky! Share, Ricky Raccoon! Share!
But not Ricky. Ricky is eating the cake.

### 5 (第 31 页)
Bella Bear is sad.
Bella Bear: "Ricky Raccoon! Where's my birthday cake?"
Oh dear, Ricky!

### 6 (第 31 页)
Ricky is at home. Ricky is sad.
Ricky Raccoon: "Grandma, my friends are angry."
Grandma Raccoon: "Ricky, are you sharing with your friends?"
Ricky Raccoon: "No ... but I like eating sweets and ice cream and cake!"

### 7 (第 32 页)
Grandma Raccoon: "Well, Ricky, your friends like sweets and ice cream and cake, too. And friends share food."
Ricky Raccoon: "Oh."
Grandma Raccoon: "Let's make some biscuits for your friends!"
Grandma and Ricky are making biscuits.

### 8 (第 32 页)
The little animals are at Ricky's house.
Ricky is giving his friends big, yummy biscuits.
Ricky is sharing!
Animals: "Thank you, Ricky! Well done, Ricky Raccoon! Well done!"

## 7 The Mouse and the Lion

### 1 (第 33 页)
One day a mouse is walking.
The mouse sees a big lion. The lion is sleeping under a tree. It's a very big lion.

### 2 (第 33 页)
The mouse jumps on the lion's tail.
Mouse: "You're very big. And I'm very small. But I'm not scared!"

### 3 (第 34 页)
Then the mouse walks on the lion's shoulders.
Mouse: "Why are the animals scared of you? I'm small, but I'm not scared. You're big, but... are you friendly?"

### 4 (第 34 页)
Then the mouse jumps on the lion's head.
The lion wakes up! He sees the small mouse.
The animals are scared.

### 5 (第 35 页)
The lion catches the mouse in his big paws.
Mouse: "Oh, please, please don't be angry, Lion. Be friendly!"
Lion: "Roar! What are you doing here, little mouse?"[?]

### 6 (第 36 页)
Mouse: "Lion, let's be friends. I'm small, but I'm a good friend. I can help you one day."
Lion: "Hmmm. OK, little mouse. I'm a friendly lion. Goodbye!"
Mouse: "Goodbye, Lion!"

### 7 (第 37 页)
The next day, the mouse hears the lion.
Lion: "Roar!"
The mouse runs to the lion. Oh no!
Lion: "Can you help me, little mouse?"
The mouse is small, but he can help. He's got sharp teeth!

### 8 (第 37 页)
Lion: "Thank you, Mouse. You're a good friend!"
Mouse: "You're welcome, Lion."
And now the small mouse and the big lion are best friends.

## 8 Sophia's Garden

### 1 (第 38 页)
It's a beautiful day. Sophia wants a garden.
She's planting flower seeds. She's planting vegetable seeds.
She is happy and excited.

### 2 (第 38 页)
The sun and the rain cloud are watching. They see Sophia planting seeds.
Sun: "I can help Sophia. Her plants need the sun to grow."
Rain Cloud: "No! I can help Sophia. Her plants need the rain to grow."

### 3 (第 39 页)
It rains and rains. The plants can't grow.
It rains in the morning and in the afternoon.
The rain cloud rains on Sophia's plants.

### 4 (第 40 页)
The sun is angry.
Sun: "Look at the plants, Rain. The plants aren't growing! Plants need sun. I can help."

### 5 (第 41 页)
The sun shines on Sophia's plants. It's sunny every day.
Sun, sun, sun ... and no rain.
The plants can't grow. Plants need rain.

### 6 (第 41 页)
Sophia is sad. The rain cloud and the sun are sad, too.
Rain Cloud: "I'm sorry. Plants need you, Sun."
Sun: "I'm sorry. Plants need you, Rain."

### 7 (第 42 页)
The sun and the rain cloud work together.
Sometimes it's sunny. Sometimes it's rainy.
Now the plants grow and grow.

### 8 (第 42 页)
Sophia is very happy. What a beautiful garden!
The sun and the rain cloud are happy, too.

## 9 Big-city Cat and Small-town Cat

### 1 (第 43 页)
Ben lives in a small town. His friend, Bill, lives in a big city.
One day, Ben visits Bill. He's excited.
Ben: "Oh! Look at the cars and the buses. There are lots of people! It's very loud."
Bill: "Hello, Ben! Welcome to the big city!"

### 2 (第 43 页)
Bill and Ben go to a restaurant. They eat some lovely food.
Bill: "Mmmm. Look out, Ben! Run!"
Ben: "Ahhh! I'm scared!"

### 3 (第 44 页)
Ben and Bill are tired. They go to bed in a shop. The bed is clean and new.
Bill: "Ooh look out, Ben! Run!"
Ben: "Ahhhh! I'm scared."

### 4 (第 44 页)
Ben: "I don't like the big city, Bill. Come to my small town."
Bill: "OK, Ben."
They catch the bus to Ben's small town.

### 5 (第 45 页)
Ben: "Welcome to my small town, Bill."
Bill: "Oh! Look at the trees and the flowers. Where are the people?" It's very quiet.
Bill: "I'm hungry. Where's dinner?"
Ben: "It's here. It's fish! Can you catch a fish?"
Bill: "Ugh! No, I can't!"

### 6 (第 46 页)
Ben and Bill are tired. They go to the park.
Bill: "Where's the bed?"
Ben: "It's here in the leaves."
Bill: "Ugh! No! I can't sleep there. It's dirty!"

### 7 (第 47 页)
Bill: "Sorry, Ben, but I don't like the small town. It's old. It's quiet. I'm bored. Let's go to the big city."
Ben: "But Bill, I don't like the big city. It's loud. It's busy. I'm scared in the city."

### 8 (第 47 页)
Ben and Bill say goodbye. Bill goes to the big city. He likes it. He's a big-city cat.
Ben stays in the small town. He likes it. He's a small-town cat.
