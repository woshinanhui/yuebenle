**12** Look at the sentences from the radio quiz. Choose the correct words. Then complete the rule.

> 1 I am / are from Birmingham.

> 3 It is / are a Spanish dish.

**RULE**：The verb be changes for different subject pronouns.

> I am Chinese.
> You/We/They are Chinese.
> He/She/It is Chinese.

We often use contracted forms after pronouns.

> I am = I'm
> You/We/They are = You're / We're / They're
> He/She/It is = He's / She's / It's

##### be

Learners often miss out **am**, **is** or **are** in sentences. We put **be** after the subject.

Learners make mistakes with word order in **be** questions. In questions, we use the order **be + subject** + object, followed with a question mark (?).
