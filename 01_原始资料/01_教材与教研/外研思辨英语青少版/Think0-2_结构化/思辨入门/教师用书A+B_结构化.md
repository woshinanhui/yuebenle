### Unit 1 Lesson 2 课时内容（Vocabulary + Grammar + Pronunciation，教师用书 T14，原书 pp 14–15）

**Warmer**：让学生尽量回忆 p12 地图上的国名，写在黑板上。

**VOCABULARY：Countries and nationalities（听力 1.02）**
- Write the country under the flag. Listen and check：Brazil | Ecuador | Indonesia | Japan | Mexico | Russia | South Africa | Spain | Sudan | Turkey | the UK | the US。
- **答案**：1 the UK 2 the US 3 Turkey 4 Japan 5 Mexico 6 Russia 7 Sudan 8 Ecuador 9 Indonesia 10 South Africa 11 Spain（+ Brazil 示例）。
- Look at Exercise 1. Complete the table with the nationalities of the countries：
  - -an/-ian：Mexican, Brazilian, Russian, Ecuadorian, Indonesian, South African, American；
  - -ish：Spanish, British, Turkish；
  - -ese：Japanese, Sudanese。

  - **In Malaysia**, you put your hand on your heart and nod your head. Sometimes you also take the person's hands in your hands first.

In the US and in other countries, some people like using a 'fist bump' to greet another person. Both people make a fist, push their arm forward slowly and touch each other's fists.
  - **In many countries around the world**, friends kiss each other on the cheek. In some countries they kiss one cheek, in others they kiss both cheeks and in some they kiss cheeks three times.
