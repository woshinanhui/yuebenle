*No, he isn't.*

*No, she isn't.*

*Yes, he is.*

5\. Is my sister young?      Yes, she is.      No, she isn't.

*Yes, she is.*

6\. Is the cat white?      Yes, it is.      No, it isn't.

*No, it isn't.*
