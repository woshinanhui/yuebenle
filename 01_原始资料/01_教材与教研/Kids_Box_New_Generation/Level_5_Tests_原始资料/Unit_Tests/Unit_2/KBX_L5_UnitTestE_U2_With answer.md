Boy: What are you going to do this weekend?

Man: Er … I’m going to make some soup.
Girl: What kind? Chicken soup?
Man: I haven’t got any chicken. I’m going to make tomato soup.
And after that, we can have some fish and salad.
3
Girl: You’re very good at drawing. Are you going to be an artist or
a video-game designer when you’re older?

to other countries and draw everything I see!
Girl: What a great life!

Boy: My friend George. Katie can’t come – she has to go to the
dentist’s.
Woman: What about Paul?
Boy: No, he doesn’t like skateboarding.
