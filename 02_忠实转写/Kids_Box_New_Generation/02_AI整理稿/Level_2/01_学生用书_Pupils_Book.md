## Unit 1 - Hello again!

**页码范围**: 4-9

### Key vocabulary

**Character names:**
- Mr Star, Mrs Star, Stella, Simon, Suzy, Grandma Star, Grandpa Star
- Marie, Maskman, Monty, Trevor

**Prepositions:**
- in, on, under

**Numbers:**
- 1-10

**The alphabet:**
- Aa-Zz

**Colours:**
- red, yellow, pink, green, orange, blue, purple, brown, black, white, grey

### Key language

- Greetings: Hello. We're the Star family.
- Who's he/she?
- This is my brother, Simon. He's seven, and this is my sister, Suzy. She's four.

### Sounds and spelling

**Consonant sound: wh (whale)**
- Example chant: "Where's the white whale? Next to the wheel!"
- Practice words: white, whale, wheel, where

### Page-by-page content

**Page 4 - Vocabulary & Language Presentation 1**
- Listen and point. Listen and repeat.
- Characters: Stella, Mrs Star, Grandma Star, Mr Star, Simon, Grandpa Star, Suzy
- Point and say: Who's she? She's Stella.
- Focus: character names, greetings, Who's he/she?

**Page 5 - Vocabulary & Language Practice 1**
- Listen and answer: Hello, I'm Trevor. Look at number four. Who's he?
- Ask and answer: Look at number three. Who's she? Stella.
- Vocabulary practice 2: numbers 1-10

**Page 6-7 - The Alphabet and Colours**
- Listen, point and repeat. Say the chant.
- Full alphabet: Aa Bb Cc Dd Ee Ff Gg Hh Ii Jj Kk Ll Mm Nn Oo Pp Qq Rr Ss Tt Uu Vv Ww Xx Yy Zz
- Play and say: Hello. I'm grey. Which letters make my sound? a, h, j, k
- Spell and guess: P-u-r-p-l-e. Purple! C-a-t. Cat!
- Put the colours in order: black, blue, brown, green, grey, orange, pink, purple, red, white, yellow

**Page 8 - Monty's Sounds and Spelling: wh**
- Watch and say: "Where's the white whale? Next to the wheel!"
- Ask and answer: The white whale is on the wheel.
- Writing practice: wh words

**Page 9 - Story: Unit language in context**
- Video story with Monty, Trevor, and characters
- Game: spelling colours and numbers
- Pencils are Trevor's favourite food
- Listen and say the number

## Grammar Reference

**页码范围**: 94-96

### Units 1, 2, 3 and 4 (Page 94)

**Who's he / she?**
- Who's he? = Who is he?
- This is my brother, Simon. He's seven.
- This is my sister, Suzy. She's four.
- he's = he is
- she's = she is

**There is / There are**
- There's a ruler. (there's = there is)
- There isn't a ruler.
- There are a lot of desks.
- There aren't a lot of desks. (there aren't = there are not)
- How many desks are there?
- Is there a whiteboard?
  - Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
- Are there ten desks?
  - Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.

**Possessive 's (Whose...)**
- Whose is this camera? It's Simon's.
- Whose are these books? They're Suzy's.
- It's Simon's. = It's Simon's camera.
- They're Suzy's. = They're Suzy's books.

**Possessive pronouns**
- Whose is that green T-shirt? It's mine.
- Whose socks are those? They're yours.
- Is that dress yours, Suzy? Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
- Are those socks yours, Simon? Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.
- Which shoes are Simon's? The grey ones are his.
- Which shoes are Stella's? The red ones are hers.
- It's mine. = It's my T-shirt.

### Units 5, 6, 7 and 8 (Page 95)

**Present continuous**
- I'm singing.
- He's / She's flying.
- You're / They're / We're flying.
- I'm not singing.
- He's not / She's not / It's not flying. (= He isn't / She isn't / It isn't)
- You're not / We're not / They're not (= You aren't / We aren't / They aren't)
- What are you doing, Suzy?
- What's Grandpa doing?

**Can I have...?**
- Can I have some meatballs, please?
- Here you are.

**Agreeing and disagreeing**
- I love donkeys.
- So do I. / I don't.

**Prepositions of place**
- Where's the park? It's behind / in front of the shops.
- Where are the flats? They're behind / in front of the shops.
- Where's the school? It's between the cafe and the park.
- Where are the shops? They're between the cafe and the park.

### Units 9, 10, 11 and 12 (Page 96)

**Wearing**
- He's / She's wearing blue shorts and white shoes.
- They're wearing sunglasses and big hats.

**Have got**
- Have you got a watch?
  - Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
- Has he / she got a watch?
  - Yes, he / she has. / No, he / she hasn't.
- haven't got = have not got
- hasn't got = has not got

**Likes and dislikes**
- I love / like / don't like swimming.
- He / She loves / likes / doesn't like playing table tennis.
- Do you like reading?
  - Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- Does he / she like reading?
  - Yes, he / she does. / No, he / she doesn't.
- doesn't = does not

**Object pronouns**
- Look at me / you / him / her / it / us / them.

**Would you like...?**
- Would you like a burger?
  - Yes, please. I'd love one.
  - No, thank you. I'd like some lemonade.
- Would you like some juice?

**Want to**
- Where do you want to go on holiday?
  - I want to go to the mountains.
- Do you want to go to a big city?
  - I don't want to go to a big city.
- Which shoes do you want?
  - I want the red ones.

## Pre A1 Starters Exam Folder

**页码范围**: 97-103

### Listening Part 2 (Page 97)

**Task type**: Listen and write a name or a number
- Question words: What? How old? What number?
- Colour coding: number questions = orange, name questions = blue
- Example questions:
  - What is your family name?
  - How old is Lucy's sister?
  - What is the name of Tom's friend?
  - What number is Alex's house?
- Task format: Two examples, then 5 questions
- Sample task: Mark Park - Eva's cousin
  - What is Mark's family name?
  - How many paintings has Mark got in his house?
  - Which park does Mark like to go to?
  - What number is Mark's house?
  - What is the name of the donkey?

### Listening Part 3 (Page 98)

**Task type 1**: Talk about the pictures. Then listen and look. Circle the correct picture.

**Task type 2**: Listen and tick the box. There is one example.
- Sample questions:
  - Which is Bill's favourite toy?
  - What sport is Anna doing this morning?
  - Where is Mrs Watson's cat?
  - What story is Grace reading?
  - What is Tom eating?
  - Which is Alex's house?

### Reading and Writing Part 1 (Page 99)

**Task type 1**: Look and read. Draw a line.
- Match words to pictures
- Examples: These are shoes. This is a train. This is a T-shirt.

**Task type 2**: Look and read. Put a tick or a cross in the box.
- Two examples, then 5 questions
- Examples: This is an apple. These are feet.
- Questions about pictures: These are shoes. This is a skirt. This is a boat. This is a train. These are hands.

### Reading and Writing Part 4 (Page 100)

**Task type 1**: Read and circle the correct word.
- Example: Jellyfish live in the park / sea.
- Sample questions:
  - This monkey has got a long tail / nose.
  - Lemons / Watermelons are green and red.
  - A kite / cat is an animal.

**Task type 2**: Read and choose a word from the box. Write the correct word.
- Topic: Cats
- Word bank: birds, animals, armchair, trees, balls, sofa, tail, house
- One example, then 5 questions
- "Many people have a cat for a pet. They are nice, beautiful animals..."

### Reading and Writing Part 5 (Page 101)

**Task type 1**: Look and read. Write 'what', 'where', 'who' or 'How many'

  - Who is the girl sitting on the bed?
  - What is the girl holding?
  - How many posters are there?

**Task type 2**: Look at the pictures and read the questions. Write one-word answers.
- Topic: Zoo / park scene
- Examples: Where are the people? in the park. What animal has the man got? dog.
- Questions:
  - What are the children doing? flying a...
  - Who is pointing? the...
  - Where is the dog? behind the...
  - Which animal has got the kite? the...
  - How many animals are there?

### Speaking Part 3 (Page 102)

**Task type**: Look and answer questions with a classmate
