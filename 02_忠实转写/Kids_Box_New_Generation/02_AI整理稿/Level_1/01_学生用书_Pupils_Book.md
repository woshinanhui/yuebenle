## Grammar reference

> Book pages 94-95

### Unit 1 — Greetings and question words

- I'm Suzy. / What's your name?
- I'm three. / How old are you?
- Contractions: I'm = I am, what's = what is

### Unit 2 — Who's that? / How are you?

- That's Alex. / Who's that?
- He's/She's seven. / How old is he/she?
- I'm fine, thank you. / How are you?
- Contractions: who's = who is, that's = that is, he's = he is, she's = she is

### Unit 3 — Prepositions of place

- It's next to the chair. / Where's your ball?
- It isn't under the table. / Is your ball in your bag?
- Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.
- Contractions: isn't = is not, it's = it is, where's = where is

### Unit 4 — Adjectives (He's / She's)

- He's happy. / He isn't ugly.

### Unit 5 — Adjectives (It's / They're)

- It's dirty. / They're long.
- Contraction: they're = they are

### Unit 6 — Have got (I / you / we)

- I / you / we've got a blue nose.
- Have you got a small mouth?
- Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
- Contractions: I've = I have, we've = we have, haven't = have not

### Unit 7 — Have got (they)

- They've got big heads. / They haven't got long legs.
- Contraction: they've = they have

### Unit 8 — Have got (he / she)

- She's got your red trousers.
- He hasn't got a blue t-shirt.
- Has he got my red trousers?
- Contraction: hasn't = has not

### Unit 9 — Can for ability

- I can ride a bike. / She can't sing.
- Can you chant?
- Contraction: can't = cannot

### Unit 10 — Present continuous (you / I)

- I'm flying. / What are you doing?
- Are you riding a bike?
- Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

### Unit 11 — Present continuous (he / she)

- What's he doing? / He's drawing a picture.
- What's she doing? / She's reading a book.

### Unit 12 — Present simple (I like / I don't like)

- I like chocolate cake. / I don't like chocolate.
- Do you like apples?
- Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
- Contraction: don't = do not
