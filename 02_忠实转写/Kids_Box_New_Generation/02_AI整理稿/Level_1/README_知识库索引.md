## 🗂️ 单元结构总览

全书共 **12 个教学单元** + 3 个复习单元 + 附录

| 单元 | 主题 | 核心词汇 | 核心语法/句型 | 发音重点 |
|------|------|----------|---------------|----------|
| **Unit 1** | Hello! | 角色名字、数字1-10、颜色 | 问候语、What's your name? / How old are you? | 首字母 'r' (red) |
| **Unit 2** | My school | 学校物品、人物名字 | Who's that? / How old is he/she? / Is he/she...? | 首字母 'p' & 'b' (pen, bag) |
| **Unit 3** | Favourite toys | 玩具、颜色(黑白灰棕) | What's your favourite...? / 介词 in, on, under, next to | 首字母 't' & 'd' (toy, doll) |
| **Unit 4** | My family | 家庭成员、形容词 | He's/She's (happy). We're (young). | 短元音 'a' (hat) |
| **Unit 5** | Our pets | 宠物、形容词(大小长短脏净) | It's (long). They're (dirty). | 短元音 'e' (hen) |
| **Unit 6** | My face | 身体部位、五官 | Have got / Has got (拥有) | 辅音 't' & 'th' (tooth, three) |
| **Unit 7** | Wild animals | 野生动物、身体部位 | They've got / They haven't got | 短元音 'i' (river) |
| **Unit 8** | My clothes | 服装 | He's/She's got / Has he/she got...? | 辅音 's' & 'sh' (spider, shoe) |
| **Unit 9** | Fun time! | 运动、休闲活动 | can / can't (能力) | 长元音 'ay', 'ai', 'ey' (day, snail, grey) |
| **Unit 10** | At the funfair | 交通工具 | 现在进行时 (What are you doing?) | 辅音 'ng' (singing) |
| **Unit 11** | Our house | 家里的房间 | 现在进行时 (What's he/she doing?) | 首字母 'm' (mouse) |
| **Unit 12** | Party time! | 食物 | 一般现在时 like / don't like | 发音复习 |
