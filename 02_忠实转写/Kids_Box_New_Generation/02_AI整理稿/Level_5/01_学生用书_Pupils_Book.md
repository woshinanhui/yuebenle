## Welcome: Welcome to our blog

### Key vocabulary

**School subjects:**
- art, computer studies, English, French, geography, German, history, maths, science, Spanish, sport, music

**School-related:**
- competition, dictionary, exam, language, lesson, prize, study, subject

### Key language

- Like / Love + -ing / nouns
- 'd like + infinitive
- Present simple questions and short answers:
  - Do you live near your school? Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

### Sounds and life skills

- **Pronunciation:** Connected speech (连读)
- **Life skill:** Communication (沟通)

### 页面内容

#### 博客介绍 (All about us)

Kid's Box is an exciting new blog for young people. Meera, Stella and Lenny want to write a post for the blog. They all go to the same school. It's called 'City School'.

**Meera:** I'm ten. I live near the school, so I walk in every day. I have lunch at school with my friends. I love drawing and taking photos, so I want to put my photos in the blog and write about the natural world.

**Stella:** I'm ten. I live in a village outside the town, so I catch a bus to school every morning. I like singing and music and I enjoy playing the piano. I love reading and studying. I'd like to write about music and science in the blog.

**Lenny:** I'm eleven years old. I always ride my bike to school. I sometimes have breakfast in the school breakfast club before my classes. I love playing football and basketball. I'm also interested in computers. I'd like to write about sport and technology in the blog.

#### 学校科目 (Our school subjects)

We all study these school subjects: maths, English, science, music, sport, art and computer studies.

- Science is an important subject, so we study it every day. This year we're learning about plants and the human body.
- Older students have to study more school subjects and take important exams.
- We study a second language. We can choose French, German or Spanish.
- In our geography lessons, we learn about different people and their countries.
- We use the dictionaries in the school library to help us understand new words.
- The best subject is history. We love learning about the past!

#### 歌曲: Because school is cool

Because school is cool, it's where we go
From Monday to Friday, I'm sure you know.
We study and we play, that's what we do.
We do it in the morning and the afternoon!

#### 发音与生活技能: Chatting with friends

**Useful phrases:**
- How are things?
- Did you have a good holiday?
- Where did you go?
- Who did you go with?

**Pronunciation focus:** Connected speech - 听力练习中注意连读现象

#### 博客: TV programmes (第12-13页)

#### 博客: London (第30-31页)

London is the capital city of England and the UK.

**Famous places:**
- **New Scotland Yard:** one of the most famous police stations in the world. A king of Scotland lived in the first Scotland Yard.
- **Red post boxes:** You can post letters or postcards at post offices or in a red postbox. They painted the first red postbox in London in 1874.
- **British Museum:** six million objects here. One of them is The Rosetta Stone.
- **Globe Theatre:** The first Globe Theatre was famous because William Shakespeare showed his plays there.
- **Tower of London:** next to Tower Bridge. It was a terrible prison for many years.
- **Black cab:** London taxi. Black-cab drivers have to pass a test to show that they know all the streets in London.
- **Heathrow Airport:** the busiest airport in the UK. More than 70 million people use this airport every year!
- **Restaurants and hotels:** London is a great city to eat out. Lots of restaurants with food from all over the world.

#### 博客: Disasters (第38-39页)

介绍了历史上一些著名的灾难：

- **The Titanic:** On 14 April 1912, it was sailing across the Atlantic Ocean when it hit an iceberg. They couldn't see the iceberg because of the fog.
- **The Hindenburg:** one of the biggest airships ever built. On 6 May 1937, when it was arriving in the USA, it caught fire. People think this happened because lightning hit it during a storm.
- **The Great Hurricane (1780):** the worst Atlantic hurricane in history, from 10-16 October 1780.
- **Lisbon earthquake:** On 1 November 1755, an earthquake hit Lisbon, in Portugal. The ground moved for ten minutes. The earthquake destroyed most of the buildings in the city.
- **Messina tsunami:** On 28 December 1908, a tsunami hit Messina, in Italy. The enormous wall of seawater was about ten metres high.
- **Krakatoa volcano:** When Krakatoa erupted on 26 August 1883, it made the loudest sound ever heard.

#### 拓展阅读: Volcanoes (第42-43页)

关于火山的地理与历史主题。

**Pompeii:** On 24 August in the year 79, nearly 2,000 years ago, a nearby volcano called Vesuvius erupted and covered the city of Pompeii in rock and ash.

**Pacific Ring of Fire:** an area in the Pacific Ocean where there are almost 75% of the Earth's volcanoes. The reason is tectonic plates (构造板块).

**Tectonic plates:** layers of rock just below the Earth's crust. The movement also causes earthquakes - around 90% happen in the Pacific Ring of Fire.

**Geothermal energy (地热能):** heat from volcanoes. People can use this energy to heat houses, make hot water and even make electricity.

#### 拓展阅读: The Science of Sound (第60-61页)

关于声波的科学主题。

**How sound is created:**
- Sounds are created when objects vibrate. These vibrations are called sound waves.
- We can't see sound waves, but they travel through the air until they reach our ears.
- If we shake a tiny bell, it vibrates quickly, so we hear a high sound.
- If we hit a large bell, the sound waves are stronger and vibrate more slowly, so we hear a low and loud sound.

**Interesting facts:**
- A baby's cry is louder than the honk of a car horn.
- The loudest sound on Earth is the sound of a volcano erupting!
- Sound needs to travel through something, like an object or air or water.
- If there is no air, like in space, there is no sound!
- Wind is air but it has no sound. It's the wind blowing against an object that makes the sound.
- Sound travels four times faster through water than air. That's how whales can hear each other even when they are hundreds of kilometres apart!

#### 博客: Nature watch (第66-67页)
