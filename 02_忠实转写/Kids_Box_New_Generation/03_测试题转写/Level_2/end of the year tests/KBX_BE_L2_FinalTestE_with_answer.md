Reading
8. Read and write. There are two extra words. There is one example.   (one point each)

Sometimes I can see big lizards on the beach and sometimes pink (3) jellyfish in the water, but I don’t like those!

My brother and my dad like playing (5) badminton or flying their kites.
There is a small café next to the beach, and sometimes we go there to get a (6) lemonade or an ice cream before we go home to the city.
