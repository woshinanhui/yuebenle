# Unit 1  How do we feel?

## Get ready

**How do we feel?**

Listen. Then point and say.

happy  happy　sad  sad　angry  angry　tired  tired

Trace and match.

## Explore

### A. Build background: Feelings

**How do we feel?**

We have different feelings.

Look and draw.

In this story, the children have different feelings in class.

*Social science*

### B. Story

**Good morning, boys and girls!**

Teacher: Good morning, boys and girls!
Children: Good morning, Miss Li!

Teacher: How are you?
Jia Li: Not so good. I'm cold.
Teacher: OK, Wen Jie. Let's close the window.

Wen Jie: I'm sad. I can't find my pencil.
Jia Li: Look! Here's a pencil.

Teacher: What about you two?
Wen Jie: I'm happy.
Jia Li: I'm happy, too!

Teacher: Well, let's start again. How are you?
Children: Fine, thank you.

Phew! Now please look at the blackboard. How do the children feel?

### C. Look and choose

Look and choose.

Here's a pencil. Let's close the window.

### D. Read and act

Not so good. I'm ...
