## 正文概览（Map of the Book）

原书前几页为「课时地图（Map of the Book）」，各单元能力目标列下（供教师/家长参考）：
- **Hello!（Unit 1，原书第 6 页）**：总复习第 1 级内容——人物、服饰、颜色、自然、物品、学校、玩具；语言 I'm (Kim). I'm a (girl). I like (books). Draw (a butterfly). Colour/Paint it (purple).；颜色 black, grey, orange, purple, white；拼读回顾字母音 a,e,i,o,u；文学 The colourful chameleon；数字复习 1–10；美术 Mixing colours（Make a colour wheel）；价值 Celebrate differences。
- **My Family（Unit 2，原书第 18 页）**：词汇 aunt, uncle, cousin, grandma, grandpa 与 funny, old, short, tall；语言 Who's that? He's my (grandpa). She's my (grandma). She's/He's/I'm (old). She isn't/He isn't/I'm not (young).；字母音 d,m；文学 Anna's baby brother；数字 11、12；科学 Growing up（Make a family album）；价值 Be patient。
- **My Home（Unit 3，原书第 30 页）**：词汇 bathroom, bedroom, dining room, kitchen, living room 与 cooking, eating, playing, sleeping; washing；语言 Where's (Kim/Dan/Dan's mummy)? She's/He's in the (kitchen). What's she/he doing? She's/He's (sleeping).；字母音 b,k；文学 Hide-and-seek；数字 13、14；数学 Shapes；价值 Be careful。
- **My Body（Unit 4，原书第 44 页）**：词汇 fingers, head, neck, shoulders, toes 与 blonde, curly, long, short, straight (hair)；语言 She's/He's/It's got (a neck). She/He/It hasn't got (short) hair.；字母音 t,n；文学 Milo's shadow；数字 One less；科学 Shadows；价值 Be resilient。
- **Outdoors（Unit 5，原书第 56 页）**：词汇 cold, hot, rainy, sunny, windy 与 boots, jumper, raincoat, sandals, sunglasses；语言 What's the weather like? It's (hot). I'm wearing (a raincoat).；字母音 s,h；文学 Rainy day fun；数字 15、16；科学 Rainbows；价值 Celebrate nature。
- **Animals（Unit 6，原书第 68 页）**：词汇 chicken, cow, goat, horse, sheep 与 fly, jump, run, swim, walk；语言 It's a (horse). It's got (a long) (neck). A (horse) can/can't (jump).；字母音 c,g；文学 Stubborn goats!；数字 More or less?；社会科学 How animals help us；价值 Be considerate。
- **My Favourite Food（Unit 7，原书第 82 页）**：词汇 burger, lolly, mango, orange, pear 与 bread, cheese, chips, eggs, fish；语言 Can I have (a pear), please? Do you like (fish)? Yes, I do. / No, I don't.；字母音 f,l,p；文学 Pea soup；数字 17、18；科学 Where food comes from；价值 Be helpful。
- **My Senses（Unit 8，原书第 94 页）**：动词 feel, hear, taste, see, smell 与 bee, grass, leaf, lemon, watermelon；语言 Can you (see) (the rain)? Yes, I can. / No, I can't. What can you (hear)? I can (hear) a (bee).；字母音 j,z；文学 A wonderful day；数字 Recognising patterns；科学 Loud and quiet；价值 Enjoy the world around you。
- **Holidays!（Unit 9，原书第 106 页）**：词汇 beach, sea, boats, kites, shells 与 drinking lemonade, eating ice cream, making sandcastles, playing with shells, taking photos；语言 How many (boats) can you see? I can see (four) (boats). I'm (playing with shells).；字母音 v,w,y；文学 You can do it, Sam!；数字 19、20；科学 Floating and sinking；价值 Persevere。
